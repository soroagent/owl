Keep your PKG safe in every browser

Sync keeps one PKG, your personal knowledge graph (your saved snippets, summaries and research), in every browser where you install soroagent: Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Brave, on any computer.

What soroagent asks Google for

  • Its own private folder in your Google Drive, used only to store your soroagent PKG. The folder is hidden and only soroagent can see it.
  • Everything in it is encrypted with your passphrase first, so not even Google can read it.
  • It can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and it gets no access to your email, contacts, photos or anything else in your Google account.

Already use soroagent in another browser? Enter the same passphrase you chose there. soroagent never stores or sends it, so keep it somewhere safe.

You can turn sync on or off any time in Settings → Sync across browsers.
