Made for people who love to read and research. soroagent puts your browser in pro mode.

Understand
Summaries shaped by the site: the accepted answer, a thread's consensus, an issue's status.
Archive
What you read, filed and tagged. Searchable by meaning, written to your Obsidian or Logseq folder.
Think
Ask questions across everything you've read. Every answer is cited to your own sources.

Private by design.

Use a local model and nothing leaves your computer, or bring your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
