soroagent

Your browser is your knowledge base.

An AI companion for every web page that turns what you read into a personal library you can think with.

Download Source code

What it does

  • Understands the page. Summaries and answers shaped by the site you're on: the accepted answer on Stack Overflow, the consensus in a Reddit thread, the status of a GitHub issue.
  • Archives what you read. Every summary is filed into your personal library with a title and tags, searchable by meaning, and can be written to your Obsidian or Logseq folder.
  • Lets you think with it. Chat with everything you've read and turn it into a brief, a study guide or a draft, with every answer cited to your own sources.
  • One library everywhere. Optional sync through your own Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted with your passphrase.

Private by design

soroagent has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your library lives in your browser. Use a model on your own computer and nothing you read leaves your machine, or bring your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key. Read the privacy policy.
