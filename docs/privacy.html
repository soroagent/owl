Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Applies to the soroagent browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox

The short version

  • soroagent has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. We (the soroagent project) never receive what you read, save or ask.
  • Everything soroagent keeps is stored in your browser, on your computer.
  • Page text and your questions go only to the AI model you choose: a model on your own computer, or a cloud AI with your own key.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync, soroagent uses only its own hidden folder in your Drive, and everything in it is encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves your browser.

1. Who we are

soroagent is an open-source browser extension (MIT license), made by soroagent.com. Its full source code is public at github.com/soroagent/owl, so anyone can check what it does with data. In this policy, "soroagent", "we" and "us" mean the extension and the project behind it.

2. What soroagent stores, and where

soroagent stores the following only in your browser's extension storage on your device:

  • Your library: summaries, saved pages, highlights, research briefs, screenshots and the notes you create, with their titles, tags and the address of the page they came from.
  • Summary history and the conversation in the panel while you use it.
  • A search index of your library (numeric "embeddings" and word counts) so you can search it by meaning.
  • Your settings, such as which AI you use, your prompts and where the owl sits.
  • Your AI API keys, if you add any. They are encrypted (AES-256) with a key the browser keeps and cannot export. They are never included in backups or sync, and Settings only ever shows their last four characters.

soroagent does not collect your browsing history. It reads a page's content only when you ask it to (Summarize, Ask, Save, Research), or, if you turn on passive capture, when you have actually read a page (by time spent and how far you scrolled). Passive capture is off by default and never captures mail, chat, banking, sign-in, checkout, account or settings pages, search results, AI chat sites or local pages.

4. Google Drive sync and Google user data

Sync is optional. You can skip it during setup and turn it on or off any time in Settings → Sync across browsers.

What soroagent accesses

When you click Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to allow one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata ("See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive"). This gives soroagent a hidden folder in your Drive that only soroagent can see.

  • soroagent cannot see, open, list or change any of your other Drive files.
  • soroagent does not request your name, email address, contacts, Gmail, calendar, photos or any other Google data.

How soroagent uses it

soroagent keeps one file there, soroagent-sync.json. It holds your library, summary history and preferences, compressed and encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your sync passphrase. Google stores only this encrypted data and cannot read it. Your API keys and screenshots are never synced. Each browser where you connect with the same passphrase reads the file, merges it with its own library and writes it back. That is the only use of this data.

Sign-in tokens

The access token Google issues is kept only in the browser's memory for the current session, never written to disk, and never sent anywhere except Google's own Drive API. Your passphrase is never stored or sent. Only a non-exportable key derived from it is kept in your browser.

Sharing

Data from your Google Drive is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models and not read by people. It never passes through any server of ours.

Limited Use

soroagent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Click Stop syncing in this browser in Settings to disconnect. This also revokes the token. You can remove soroagent's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and delete its hidden data in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → soroagent → Delete hidden app data.

5. Files soroagent writes on your computer

  • Automatic backup: by default, a backup of your library and settings (never your API keys) is kept in your Downloads folder under soroagent/, so you can restore it after a reinstall. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Markdown folder (optional): if you choose a folder, such as an Obsidian vault, soroagent writes your notes there as .md files. In Firefox these go to Downloads/soroagent/notes.

6. Browser permissions and why soroagent needs them

  • Read and change data on websites: to show the owl and read the page you ask about (and, if you turn it on, pages you read for passive capture), and to reach your AI provider.
  • Storage: to keep your library and settings in your browser.
  • Downloads: for backups, exports and the Firefox Markdown folder.
  • Identity: to open Google's own sign-in window when you connect Google Drive.
  • Context menus, scripting, alarms and active tab: for right-click actions, the panel, and periodic sync and backup.

7. Your control over your data

  • You can view, search, export and delete any note in the Snippets page, and export or delete everything in Settings → Backup & restore.
  • Uninstalling soroagent deletes everything it stored in that browser. Backups in your Downloads folder and any sync file stay until you delete them.
  • Because we hold none of your data, we cannot see it, recover it or hand it to anyone. If you lose your sync passphrase, the synced copy cannot be decrypted. Your local library is unaffected.

8. Children

soroagent is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If soroagent's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and note it in the history of this page.
